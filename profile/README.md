# German Wear GmbH

**Fashion, e-commerce, and ERP technology from Hannover, Germany.**

German Wear GmbH develops and operates B2B fashion and apparel businesses with a strong focus on e-commerce, wholesale, and process automation. We combine commercial operations with in-house software engineering to connect marketplaces, logistics, and enterprise resource planning on a single, maintainable platform.

## What we do

- **Fashion and apparel** — product, sourcing, and multi-channel sales
- **E-commerce and wholesale** — B2B and consumer-facing digital commerce
- **Marketplace integrations** — connecting external sales channels to internal operations
- **ERP and Odoo** — customized Odoo-based enterprise solutions for order, inventory, and finance flows
- **Shipping and automation** — carrier integrations and operational workflows that scale with volume

## Technology and partnerships

Our engineering teams build and operate integrations between **Odoo ERP** and external marketplaces and carriers. Development follows documented engineering standards, structured issue tracking, and controlled release paths from development through staging to production.

We work with established Odoo and cloud hosting practices suitable for regulated, multi-environment ERP operations.
